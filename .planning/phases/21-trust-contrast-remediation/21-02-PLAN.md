## Anti-Patterns & Risk Controls

- **Recharts SVG Gradient Defs**: Notice that SVG `<linearGradient>` elements must receive dynamic hex colors in `stopColor` since SVG `stop-color` attributes with CSS variables can fail in SVG export or specific browser engines. `chartTheme.colors.*` supplies exact resolved hexes dynamically.
- **Resize & Theme Toggle Synchronization**: The `MutationObserver` on `data-theme` ensures instant re-render upon theme toggling without needing page reloads.
