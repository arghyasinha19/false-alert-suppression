## Objective

Remediate inverted and failing CSS color tokens across light and dark themes to ensure strict WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance: correct `--text-tertiary` to `#64748b` in light mode (4.64:1 contrast against `#ffffff`) and `#94a3b8` in dark mode (5.45:1 contrast against `#111827`); introduce dedicated semantic tokens `--badge-blue-text: #1d4ed8;` (light) and `--badge-blue-text: #93c5fd;` (dark); update `.badge.backdated`, `.badge.snow-new`, `.badge.badge-subtle.blue`, and filter pills to bind to `--badge-blue-text` (delivering 7.32:1 and 9.81:1 contrast); and establish automated WCAG mathematical contract tests.

## Anti-Patterns & Risk Controls

- **Do NOT globally alter `--accent-blue`** — changing `--accent-blue` in light mode would affect primary action buttons, active navigation indicators, and focus rings. Keep `--accent-blue` at `#2563eb` and apply `--badge-blue-text: #1d4ed8` specifically to text on badges.
- **Strict mathematical validation** — Ensure luminance formulas account for sRGB gamma expansion ($C / 12.92$ vs $((C + 0.055) / 1.055)^{2.4}$).
